# Plan 14-01: Paper Trading Engine

## Outcome
Implemented a realistic Paper Trading Engine that processes orders generated by the OMS. It correctly models latency jitter (with adverse price drift), asset-class specific commissions (US, Indian, Crypto), dynamic slippage, and square-root market impact for oversized orders. The engine safely evaluates limit orders against candle highs/lows.
